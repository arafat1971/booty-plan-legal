Privacy Policy — Booty Plan

Last updated: 2026-10-22

Booty Plan ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

The app does not sell personal information, and you can use all of it without an account.

On your device. Your name (optional), goal, workouts, logged sets, meals, water, habits, badges, weekly check-ins (weight, body measurements, ratings) and progress photos are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Optional account and cloud backup. If you create an account (with email and password, or with Google sign-in), we store your email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account name and ID, and a backup of your progress (everything above except photos) with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), so you can restore it on another phone. Only you can read your backup. You can delete the account and the backup at any time in the app: You tab > Cloud backup > Delete account. You can also email us at arafathossain455@gmail.com to ask for deletion.

Usage and crash data. The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect an app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version, approximate region, which screens and features are used (for example "session finished"), and crash logs. They never receive your name, measurements, photos or what you typed. You can switch this off in the app: You tab > Cloud backup > Share anonymous usage and crash data.

Health Connect (optional). If you switch on Health Connect in the You tab, the app writes your finished sessions to Health Connect as strength workouts and reads your step count for today to tick the "8,000 steps" habit. Step counts are used on your device only and are not sent to us or to Firebase. The app requests only these two permissions: write exercise and read steps. You can switch this off in the app or remove access in the Health Connect app at any time. Our use of Health Connect data follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Push messages. The app registers a Firebase Cloud Messaging token so we can send occasional announcements. Training reminders are scheduled on your device and do not use this.

Anonymous diagnostics (camera rep counter only): The optional camera rep counter uses Google's ML Kit library. When you use it, ML Kit may send anonymous technical data to Google over HTTPS: a per-installation identifier, app version, device model and OS version, and performance and error information. This data is used for diagnostics and analytics, is not linked to your identity, and is not shared with third parties by us. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure for details.

Camera and photos

  • Rep counter: If you choose "Count with camera", the app uses your camera to detect body position and count reps. Video is processed on your device only. It is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Progress photos: Photos you add in "Progress photos" are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. Deleting a photo, or uninstalling the app, removes it.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. These reminders send no data to any server.

Purchases

Week 1 of the plan is free. The rest is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play (earlier versions were a paid download, and people who bought those keep the full plan). There are no subscriptions. Google Play handles the payment and tells the app only whether the plan was bought; we do not receive or store your payment details. Whether you have the full plan is saved on your device and, if you have an account, in your cloud backup. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

Health disclaimer

The app provides general fitness and healthy-eating ideas, not medical advice.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change.
