Delete your Booty Plan account

For the Booty Plan app on Google Play

An account is optional. It only exists if you signed up for cloud backup. Deleting it removes the account and the cloud backup for good.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open Booty Plan and go to the You tab.
  • Tap Cloud backup, then Delete account, and confirm.
  • The account and its backup are deleted straight away.

By email (no app needed)

Email arafathossain455@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, or tell us that address, and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, and for Google sign-in your Google account name and ID.
  • Your cloud backup: workouts, meals, habits, check-ins, measurements and settings.

What is kept

  • Nothing linked to your account. Anonymous usage and crash data is not tied to your account or email; Google Analytics and Crashlytics delete it automatically after their set retention period.
  • Progress photos never leave your phone. Data on your phone stays there until you use Reset all progress in the app or uninstall it.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play, which keeps its own records.
